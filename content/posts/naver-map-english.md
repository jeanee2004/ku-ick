Google Maps barely works in Korea — public transit routes are patchy, and a lot of business listings simply aren't there. Everyone who actually lives here, expats included, ends up on **Naver Map** instead. The catch: it opens in Korean by default, which is a rough first impression if you don't read Hangul yet.

The fix takes under a minute, and it's an iOS system setting, not something buried in the app itself.

**Quick answer:** On iPhone, the language Naver Map displays in is controlled from your phone's Settings app, not from inside Naver Map. Go to Settings → Naver Map → Language → English, then reopen the app.

## Why This App, Specifically

Naver Map is the most accurate service in Korea for directions, public transit info, and place search — more reliable here than Google Maps by a wide margin. Once it's in English, you get a fully navigable transit and walking-directions app for day-to-day life.

## Step 1 — Change the Language in iOS Settings

- Open the iPhone **Settings** app.
- Scroll down and select **Naver Map** from the list of installed apps.
- Tap **Language** at the very bottom of that page.
- Select **English**.

## Step 2 — Verify It Worked

- Reopen the Naver Map app.
- Check that the main menus — including **Settings → Language** inside the app itself — now show English.
- Try a search in English and a public transit route to confirm everything carried over.

## Tips & Precautions

- **Some places only exist in Korean.** Small local shops or very specific addresses sometimes only appear under their Korean name. Keeping a destination's Korean name or phone number on hand makes it much easier to find, even with the app set to English.
- **Android users:** the same idea applies — the language for an individual app is usually controlled through your phone's own app-language settings rather than inside Naver Map — but the exact menu path varies by device and Android version, so check your phone's settings app under app-specific language options.

## The Bottom Line

This is a two-minute fix that changes your whole day-to-day experience of getting around Korea. Do it the moment you install the app, before you actually need directions somewhere.
