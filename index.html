    FAQ

    Common questions, grouped by when they come up. General guidance — always confirm campus-specific details with the Office of International Affairs.

    Before You Arrive

    Do I need a Korean phone number before I arrive?

    No — most students get a prepaid USIM after landing (see Life in Korea → Mobile). Check with your home carrier about short-term international roaming for the first day or two.

    How do I apply for a dorm room?

    Dorm applications are handled through the university before arrival — check the current cycle on the KU Sejong portal (see Community → Official KU Channels).

    What should I pack that's hard to find in Korea?

    Prescription medication in original packaging with a doctor's note, spare glasses/contacts, and a plug adapter — Korea uses Type C/F sockets at 220V.

    First Week

    What's the first thing I should do after landing?

    Settle into your dorm/housing, then plan your ARC application within the required window (see Life in Korea → Immigration), and get a prepaid SIM if you don't have connectivity yet.

    Where do I get my student ID?

    At the Library (Academic Information Center) — see the Map.

    Is tap water safe to drink on campus?

    Bottled or filtered water is the common default in Korea and varies by building — check dorm signage, and use a filter or boiled water if you're unsure.

    Ongoing Life

    What do I do in a medical emergency?

    Call 119 (fire & ambulance) — see Emergency & Safety.

    What if I lose my ARC or passport?

    Report a stolen passport/ARC to the police (112), then contact your embassy for passport reissue and the immigration office (HiKorea, 1345) for ARC reissue.

    How do I ask for help in Korean-only situations?

    Papago's camera/voice translation covers most day-to-day situations — or ask KUS-Buddy / the Office of International Affairs. See Useful Apps and the "Need help?" banner.

    Privacy Policy

    Last updated: August 2026

    KU-ICK is a static, unofficial student-made site for Korea University Sejong Campus international students. It is not affiliated with Korea University's official portal.

    What we store

    This site has no server or database. If you sign up or log in, your name, email, nationality, and student type are saved only in your own browser's local storage (localStorage) — they are never transmitted anywhere. Clearing your browser data or using "Delete account" in My Page removes it completely.

    Accounts are a demo

    Sign-up and log-in on this site do not verify any credential (no password check, no email confirmation). Treat it as a convenience for saving places/guides in your browser, not a real account system, until a real backend is added.

    Cookies & tracking

    This site does not set cookies and does not currently run any analytics or tracking scripts.

    External links

    Map directions, Useful Apps, and Life in Korea guides link out to third-party sites and apps (Kakao Map, Naver Map, Google Maps, HiKorea, NHIS, banking apps, delivery apps, etc.). Those services have their own privacy policies — this policy only covers KU-ICK itself.
